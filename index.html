EVERY DEX ENTRY

Every Pokémon, one Dex entry at a time.

Every Dex Entry is an unofficial fan channel that covers each Pokémon in National Pokédex order, one short video per entry, starting with Kanto.

Every Dex Entry Publisher

Every Dex Entry Publisher is the tool the channel uses to upload its own finished videos to its own YouTube channel, and to read that channel's video statistics. It is operated only by the channel's owner. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

When the owner signs in with Google, the tool asks for permission to upload videos to the channel, to view the channel and its videos, and to view the channel's YouTube Analytics reports. How that access is used is set out in the privacy policy, and the conditions of use are in the terms of service.
